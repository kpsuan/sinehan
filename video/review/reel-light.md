# Review: reel-light.mp4

The light cut mostly works. It is 60.0 s, 1920x1080 at 30 fps, with AAC stereo audio. The cream-to-sand gradient, near-black headlines and one accent colour per section (orange, green, orange, magenta, violet) give it a warm, clear look, and the section numbering "01 make it look good" through "06 off the clock" makes the story easy to follow. Every headline, card label and contact chip is easy to read at full size, and I found no typos. One thing must be fixed before delivery. In the A Whole Family Matter scene (about 34.8 to 37.3 s), the browser mock-up scrolls past the end of its screenshot, so the bottom third of the window turns into a solid near-black block. It is the most noticeable flaw in the film, and it is exactly the kind of dark-mode leftover that stands out on a light background. The other issues are polish: some pale secondary text, a few awkward line wraps, one long-ish hold in the Sinehan scene, and a faint seam during one push transition. Audio is clean. Music plays from start to finish, nothing clips, and the last note rings out naturally to silence.

Approximate scene timings (checked with -ss): hero 0 to 7.0, 01 look good 7.0 to 15.0, 02 work 15.0 to 22.9, 03 move 23.0 to 30.9, 04 now 31.0 to 37.3, 05 for fun 37.3 to 46.4, 06 off the clock 46.5 to 52.4, end card 52.5 to 60.0.

Fix by stopping the scroll before the content ends, using a taller capture, or cutting to the next scene earlier. — Fixed: both screenshots now fill the browser window (height 100%, cover); checked at 36 s.
- [x] YELLOW 39.5 to 46.4: In the Sinehan scene, the metadata line "Chrome extension · WebRTC · a dog who does tricks" is light grey on cream (roughly #A9A3A3 on #F6F1EA), well below comfortable contrast. It also wraps with "tricks" alone on the second line. Darken it to the body-copy grey and widen the text box or shorten the line. — Fixed: darker grey, one line, smaller size; checked at 44 s.
- [x] YELLOW 37.3 to 46.4: The Sinehan scene runs about 9 s and is fully built by about 41.5 s. Apart from the dog's spin and the "Wheee!" pop around 41 to 43 s, roughly 43.5 to 46.4 is a static hold, the closest thing to a dead hold in the film.
