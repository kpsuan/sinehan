Just the two of you
no accounts, nothing tracked.

No sign-up, no email, no location. You pick a name and a picture, and they stay in your browser.

Private
No account

A name and a link is all it takes to start or join. Nothing to sign up for, no password to forget.

No location

Nobody sees where you are. Sinehan never asks for your location or contacts, and there's no ad profile.

Nothing kept

Your chat stays on your laptop. The server only passes messages between you two, and calls go straight from one browser to the other.

Set up in a minute
no account needed.

Sinehan isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's added by hand. It only takes three steps.
