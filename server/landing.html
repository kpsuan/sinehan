Everything for movie night

Each of you watches on your own account. Sinehan handles being together.

⏯️

Synced playback

Play, pause and skip on either side and the other follows. A 3·2·1 countdown starts you at the same moment.

💬

Chat & reactions

Messages carry the moment they were sent. Tap one to jump back. Emoji float across the video.

📹

Video call

See each other in the sidebar, or as a floating window you can drag around in fullscreen.

⏸️

Waits for you

Ads, buffering, a dropped connection: the other side pauses until you're both back.

🔗

One-link invites

Send a link. They join the party and land on the same show, at the same spot.

🕘

Past parties

Each night's chat is saved with what you watched, so you can look back on it.

Works where you watch

Any site with a video player in Chrome.

YouTubeNetflixPrime VideoDisney+MaxHulu…and more

You each need your own account on the service. Ad detection on some services is best-effort.

Install in a minute

Sinehan isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's added by hand.

Download & unzip

Download the zip and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents.

Load it into Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the sinehan folder.

Start a party

Pin Sinehan, open a show, click the 🔗 in the sidebar and send the link. That's it.
