## Description

Sinehan is a movie theater for two. Open the same show in two places and watch it together, in sync.

Learn more: https://sinehan.live/

- Synced playback: play, pause or skip on either side and the other follows. A 3·2·1 countdown starts you at the same moment.
- Waits for each other: if one of you hits an ad, a slow connection or a dropped Wi-Fi, the other pauses until you're back.
- Chat while you watch: messages, reactions and timestamps you can click to jump to that scene, even in fullscreen.
- Voice or video call, right beside the show. It keeps going when you go fullscreen.
- One invite link: they open it and land on the same show and scene.
- Watch guides: the episodes you need for a story, or a franchise's films in order. Save one and it's on their side too, with a checklist you both tick.
- Tonight: can't decide? Each of you swipes through shows and films that stream where you live, and a show you both like is a match.
- Who did it? Lock in a guess about the culprit; it stays sealed until you both reveal.
- Your phone as a remote and chat, no install needed.
- CR breaks, "stop for tonight" (pick up where you left off), and Bantay, the dog who keeps watch.

Works on most sites with a video player. Each of you needs your own access to the show.

Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Chats aren't stored on our server; calls go directly between you.

## Permission justifications (Privacy practices tab)

- **offscreen** (new in 2.13): runs the voice/video call in a hidden page, so the call keeps going when Chrome closes or reloads the side panel (for example when the video goes fullscreen) and plays the other person's voice there.
- **tabs**: knows which tab has the show, follows it when it changes episode, and opens the show from an invite link.
- **sidePanel**: the app lives in Chrome's side panel next to the show.
- **storage**: remembers your name, settings, saved watch guides and past parties on your computer.
- **notifications**: tells you when the other person calls while the side panel is closed.
- **clipboardWrite**: copies the invite link.
- **Host permissions (all sites)**: the show can be on any site with a video player, so Sinehan needs to see and sync that site's video (play, pause, position).
